Cooper Thomas Compliance Tracker aka CTCT

This site gives Cooper's loved ones—especially his mom, Gramps, and close family—a clear place to help track court requirements, weekly responsibilities, documents, payments, appointments, treatment, testing, and community-service progress.

Open Compliance TrackerHow this site helps

One shared place for the people supporting him.

The goal is not to judge or punish Cooper. It is to reduce confusion, prevent missed requirements, preserve proof of completion, and make it easier for everyone helping him to stay on the same page.

Track every requirement

Review court-ordered conditions, mark progress, record due dates, and keep notes about anything that still needs attention.

Build a weekly routine

Use suggested weekly tasks for reporting, community service, payments, treatment, testing, attorney communication, and family review.

Keep proof together

Record receipts, appointment confirmations, test results, service-hour sheets, class certificates, and written communications with supervision.

Ready to review Cooper's current plan?

The tracker saves entries in the browser and can be printed or saved as a PDF for family meetings.
